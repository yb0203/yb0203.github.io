## ✉️ 3. Cold Outreach Pitch Templates

Founder's Office and high-agency PM roles are often unlisted. Cold outreaches using short, high-agency messages are highly effective.

### Template A: Founder's Office (High-Agency Builder)
> **Subject**: Engineer-adjacent builder for [Company Name] (IIT-BHU / Fintech Core Dev)
>
> Hi [Founder Name],
>
> I saw [Company Name] is scaling [mention a recent launch, funding round, or company focus].
>
> I’m a developer at Kotak Mahindra Bank who has shipped core platforms (handling high-volume cash transactions) and built organizational AI safety evaluation standards. Beyond enterprise work, I build 0-to-1 tools in the open—like a natural-language Git MCP server [Vibe Check] that enables non-technical teammates to manage complex branches.
>
> I want to bring this builder mindset to your team as a Founder’s Office associate, taking on whatever problems (from compliance to AI agent engineering) prevent your team from moving fast. 
>
> Do you have 10 minutes next week for a quick chat?
>
> Best,
