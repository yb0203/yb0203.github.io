# 4A. Functional Head Mapping

## ⚡ TL;DR
- Five roles control the budget and approval of legal technology inside a bank.
- The Chief Legal Officer cares about overall budget, risk exposure, and speed of delivery.
- Risk and compliance heads focus heavily on preventing data leaks and meeting federal regulatory requirements.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Stakeholder Profiles
1. **Chief Legal Officer (CLO) / General Counsel**
   - **Workflows Owned**: W1 (Transactional), W3 (Litigation), W4 (Risk), W5 (Ops).
   - **Key Motivation**: Lower external litigation spend, reduce contract turnaround times, manage board-level legal risk.
   - **Success Metric**: Average contract negotiation cycle time; annual law firm billings.
   - **Primary Fear**: Regulatory compliance failures or public litigation setbacks.

2. **Head of Litigation**
   - **Workflows Owned**: W3 (Litigation & Dispute).
   - **Key Motivation**: Prevent ex-parte orders, optimize panel counsel allocation, manage massive court date lists.
   - **Success Metric**: Case resolution rate; percentage of dates tracked without manual errors.
   - **Primary Fear**: A missing court date that results in an automated default judgement against the bank.

3. **Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**
   - **Workflows Owned**: W2 (Regulatory & Compliance).
   - **Key Motivation**: Maintain clean audits with federal regulators; identify compliance gaps across internal policy manuals.
   - **Success Metric**: Number of audit findings per quarter; speed of circular implementation.
   - **Primary Fear**: Getting fined by federal regulators for missing a regulatory update.

4. **Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**
   - **Workflows Owned**: W4 (Risk & Advisory).
   - **Key Motivation**: Standardize risk assessment processes; restrict data access to secure local networks.
   - **Success Metric**: Operational risk index; zero data residency audit failures.
   - **Primary Fear**: Financial customer details leaking to external cloud LLM providers.

5. **Head of Legal Operations**
   - **Workflows Owned**: W5 (Legal Operations).
   - **Key Motivation**: Streamline software tools, verify billing accuracy, manage document stores.
   - **Success Metric**: User adoption rates; software ROI; SLA compliance.
   - **Primary Fear**: Buying complex software that the internal legal team refuses to use.
