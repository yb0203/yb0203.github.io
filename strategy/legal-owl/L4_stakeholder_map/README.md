# Layer 4: Stakeholder Map — Executive Summary

## ⚡ TL;DR
- The Buying Committee in large private banks consists of 5 key functional heads (CLO, Head of Litigation, CCO, CRO, Head of Legal Ops).
- The Chief Legal Officer (CLO) serves as the primary sponsor, while the Chief Risk Officer (CRO) acts as the primary gatekeeper.
- Trust and data security are the most critical adoption barriers, requiring targeted objection-handling scripts.
