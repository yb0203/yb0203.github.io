# 4B. Decision Matrix

## ⚡ TL;DR
- Legal technology deals require approvals across the entire enterprise buying committee.
- The CLO is the economic buyer, while IT Security holds absolute veto power.
- The DACI framework outlines roles for each department to ensure deals do not stall.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Buying Committee Roles
- **Economic Buyer**: Chief Legal Officer (CLO). Controls the department budget.
- **Technical Buyer / Gatekeeper**: Head of IT Security & Infrastructure. Verifies data isolation.
- **End User**: Legal Managers, Litigation Officers, Compliance Auditors.
- **Influencer**: External Law Firms / Panel Counsel.

### DACI Matrix for Legal Owl Purchase
| Stage | Driver (D) | Approver (A) | Contributor (C) | Informed (I) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Pilot Approval** | Head of Legal Ops | Chief Legal Officer | Litigation Head | Legal Teams |
| **IT Security Review** | Head of Legal Ops | Head of IT Security | CRO / Compliance | CLO |
| **Commercial Contract** | Head of Legal Ops | Procurement Head | Chief Legal Officer | Finance Team |
| **Production Rollout** | Legal Ops / SDE | Chief Legal Officer | End Users | IT Ops |

### Managing the Veto
IT Security is the most common source of deal slippage in banking sales cycles. To prevent delays, we introduce a standardized **"Security Deployment Package"** during the first discovery call. This package details our offline containerized VPC model, satisfying IT requirements before they request a formal review.
