# 6D. Product Roadmap

## ⚡ TL;DR
- A 4-phase roadmap guides the evolution of Legal Owl from a niche tool to an enterprise platform.
- Phase 1 (MVP) focus is strictly on date tracking and contract playbooks for private banks.
- Phase 2 expands capabilities to include compliance auditing and regional OCR processing.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Phased Roadmap Timeline

#### Phase 1: MVP & Verification (0 - 3 Months)
- **Goal**: Validate core value proposition and secure initial banking reference accounts.
- **Key Features**: Local Docker VPC deployment; eCourts daily scraping engine; template deviation highlighting; citation links.
- **Target Metrics**: 70%+ user adoption; zero missed court dates; under 2 minutes for deviation checks.
- **Business Milestone**: Complete 3 bank pilots; secure first annual recurring revenue (ARR) contract.

#### Phase 2: Platform Expansion (3 - 9 Months)
- **Goal**: Expand user seats and introduce compliance auditing tools.
- **Key Features**: RBI Policy Auditor; regional language OCR engine; SSO integration; custom playbook builder.
- **Target Metrics**: 50% increase in active users; under 30 minutes to review RBI compliance manuals.
- **Business Milestone**: Standardize sales sequences; convert 5 additional private banks/NBFCs.

#### Phase 3: Segment Diversification (9 - 18 Months)
- **Goal**: Expand from banking to corporate law firms and consultancies.
- **Key Features**: Panel Counsel billing auditing; automated land title chain reconstruction; internal legal FAQ chatbot.
- **Target Metrics**: Under 2 hours to summarize land titles; billing audit error identification rate > 95%.
- **Business Milestone**: Launch self-serve SaaS portal; acquire 20 corporate law firms.

#### Phase 4: Enterprise Ecosystem (18 Months+)
- **Goal**: Establish Legal Owl as the pan-India legal operations infrastructure standard.
- **Key Features**: Automated document ingestion API integrations; advanced collaborative editing suite; multi-tenant compliance networks.
- **Target Metrics**: Over 100 enterprise customers; 95% retention rate.
- **Business Milestone**: International expansion scoping (targeting other SE Asian markets with similar regulatory structures).
