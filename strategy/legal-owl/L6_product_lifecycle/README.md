# Layer 6: Product Lifecycle — Executive Summary

## ⚡ TL;DR
- The Phase 1 MVP is focused on a single target ICP (large private banks) and a single core pain point (eCourts court date tracking).
- Clear, metric-driven KPIs are established for each module to measure and prove ROI to banking customers.
- We establish an agile product loop to capture in-app feedback and feed it directly into the prioritized backlog.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Product Evolution Strategy
Legal Owl evolves from an automation tool into an integrated platform. By starting with a highly focused MVP (litigation tracking and deviation audits), we build the security trust required to expand into compliance and automated risk auditing.
