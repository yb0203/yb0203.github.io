### Glossary of Key Strategy Terms
1. **ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**: The specific archetype of enterprise most likely to buy Legal Owl in Phase 1 (e.g., large private banks with 20-80 legal headcount).
2. **GTM (Go-To-Market)**: The tactical commercial motion detailing how we acquire leads, move them through the sales cycle, and win contracts.
3. **MoSCoW**: A prioritization framework grouping backlog items into Must-have, Should-have, Could-have, and Won't-have.
4. **RBI (Reserve Bank of India)**: The central banking regulator whose circulars dictate IT security, compliance, and outsourcing rules.
5. **CLO (Chief Legal Officer)**: The head of the legal department, also referred to as General Counsel (GC).
6. **CCO (Chief Compliance Officer)**: Head of the compliance team, focused on regulatory alignment and RBI audits.
7. **CRO (Chief Risk Officer)**: Head of risk, evaluating data safety, liability, and operational risk.
8. **POC (Proof of Concept)**: A short-term (14-30 days) product trial designed to validate core technical capabilities in a customer's environment.
9. **NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Company)**: A financial institution that offers banking services without holding a full banking license.
10. **SLA (Service Level Agreement)**: Contractual timelines for resolving issues or processing workflows.
11. **Abstention Primitives**: Core AI engine rules that force the model to refuse to answer rather than hallucinating when information is missing.
