# Layer 2: Problem Space — Executive Summary

## ⚡ TL;DR
- Banking legal tasks decompose into 5 MECE workflow categories (Transactional, Regulatory, Litigation, Risk Advisory, Ops).
- The highest pain points cluster in **Regulatory Compliance (W2)** and **Transactional Legal (W1)** due to volume and the threat of regulatory penalties.
- Litigation and Dispute (W3) represents a secondary high-frequency area, currently tracked manually on spreadsheets.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Problem Space Map
Banking legal operations are bottlenecked by manual document review. A single large bank reviews thousands of pages of loan agreements, regulatory circulars, and court notices daily. These tasks are repetitive, error-prone, and lead to massive operational backlogs.
