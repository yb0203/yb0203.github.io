# 1A. Industry Segmentation

## ⚡ TL;DR
- Large Private Banks (e.g., Kotak, ICICI, HDFC) are selected as the phase-1 anchor ICP.
- PSU Banks have high litigation volume but long, complex RFP/tender processes (12-18 months) that drain early-stage startup resources.
- NBFCs are agile but have smaller budgets and lower overall legal department headcount.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Banking Archetypes Matrix
| Segment | Legal Team Size | Decision Speed | AI Readiness | Budget Range (Annual) | Procurement Method | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Large Private** (Kotak, HDFC, ICICI) | 40 - 100 lawyers | Medium (3-6 mo) | High (Active AI trials) | INR 2 Cr - 5 Cr | Direct PO / Pilot to contract | **Tier 1 (Anchor)** |
| **Mid Private** (Yes Bank, Federal Bank) | 15 - 40 lawyers | Fast (2-4 mo) | Medium | INR 50L - 1.5 Cr | Direct PO | **Tier 2 (Fast Follow)** |
| **PSU Banks** (SBI, Bank of Baroda) | 150 - 500+ lawyers | Very Slow (12-18 mo)| Low (Legacy systems) | INR 5 Cr - 15 Cr | Public Tender / RFP | **Tier 3 (Wait)** |
| **NBFCs** (Bajaj Finance, Tata Capital) | 10 - 30 lawyers | Fast (1-3 mo) | High | INR 30L - 1 Cr | Direct PO | **Tier 2 (Fast Follow)** |
| **Cooperative Banks** (SVC, Saraswat) | 2 - 10 lawyers | Slow | Low | INR 5L - 20L | Committee approval | **Tier 4 (Ignore)** |

### Recommended Anchor ICP Justification
Large Private Banks present the optimal balance of scale, budget, and transaction volume without the administrative overhead of PSU public tenders. These institutions actively look for tools to automate their high-volume compliance checks and litigation management, making them the ideal sandbox to pilot Legal Owl.
