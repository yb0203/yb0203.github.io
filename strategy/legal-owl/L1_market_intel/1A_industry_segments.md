# 1A. Industry Segmentation

## ⚡ TL;DR
- Large National Banks (e.g., Chase, Wells Fargo, Bank of America) are selected as the phase-1 anchor ICP.
- G-SIBs / Megabanks have high litigation volume but long, complex RFP/tender processes (12-18 months) that drain early-stage startup resources.
- Fintechs are agile but have smaller budgets and lower overall legal department headcount.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Banking Archetypes Matrix
| Segment | Legal Team Size | Decision Speed | AI Readiness | Budget Range (Annual) | Procurement Method | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Large National** (Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo) | 40 - 100 lawyers | Medium (3-6 mo) | High (Active AI trials) | $250,000 - $600,000 | Direct PO / Pilot to contract | **Tier 1 (Anchor)** |
| **Regional Banks** (Fifth Third Bank, Citizens Bank) | 15 - 40 lawyers | Fast (2-4 mo) | Medium | $75,000 - $200,000 | Direct PO | **Tier 2 (Fast Follow)** |
| **G-SIBs / Megabanks** (JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo) | 150 - 500+ lawyers | Very Slow (12-18 mo)| Low (Legacy systems) | $600,000 - $2,000,000 | Public Tender / RFP | **Tier 3 (Wait)** |
| **Fintechs** (SoFi, Chime) | 10 - 30 lawyers | Fast (1-3 mo) | High | $40,000 - $125,000 | Direct PO | **Tier 2 (Fast Follow)** |
| **Credit Unions & Small Community Banks** (Navy Federal Credit Union & local community banks) | 2 - 10 lawyers | Slow | Low | $5,000 - $25,000 | Committee approval | **Tier 4 (Ignore)** |

### Recommended Anchor ICP Justification
Large National Banks present the optimal balance of scale, budget, and transaction volume without the administrative overhead of G-SIB formal RFPs. These institutions actively look for tools to automate their high-volume compliance checks and litigation management, making them the ideal sandbox to pilot the Platform.
