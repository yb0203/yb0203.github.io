# 5D. Commercial Model & Pricing

## ⚡ TL;DR
- The Platform is priced under a tiered Annual Subscription model, billed in USD.
- Pilots are structured as paid engagements ($5,000 for 30 days) to filter for high-intent buyers.
- Pricing tiers range from a basic Starter package to a custom Enterprise VPC plan.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Pricing Model Evaluation
- **Per-Seat Licensing (Recommended)**: Best for contract reviews (W1) as it maps directly to legal team headcount.
- **Volume-Based (Alternative)**: Best for PACER & state court dockets tracking (W3) or federal regulatory auditing (W2) as it maps to the number of active cases or document volume.
- **Hybrid Model (Selected)**: Base platform fee + per-user seat fee. This ensures predictable baseline revenue while capturing expansion upsell.

### Commercial Pricing Tiers (USD / Year)
1. **Starter Tier (Mid Fintechs / Boutiques)**
   - *Target*: Legal teams of 5-10 users.
   - *Scope*: Litigations up to 500 cases; standard contract templates.
   - *Price*: **$15,000 per year** (SaaS or Private Cloud).
   - *Support*: Slack & email support (24hr SLA).

2. **Growth Tier (Regional Banks)**
   - *Target*: Legal teams of 10-25 users.
   - *Scope*: Litigations up to 2,000 cases; custom playbooks; SSO integration.
   - *Price*: **$35,000 per year** (Private AWS/Azure VPC).
   - *Support*: Dedicated account manager (4hr SLA).

3. **Enterprise Tier (Large National Banks)**
   - *Target*: Legal teams of 25-80+ users.
   - *Scope*: Unlimited cases; multiple playbooks; Federal Policy Auditor; local server installations.
   - *Price*: **$75,000+ per year** (On-Premise / Air-Gapped).
   - *Support*: 24/7 dedicated support (1hr SLA).

### Pilot Structure
We do not run free pilots. Free trials often lack stakeholder commitment and stall.
- **Pilot Fee**: **$5,000** (One-time, 30 days).
- **Deliverables**: Sandbox installation; onboarding for up to 5 users; up to 200 cases tracked; 2 custom contract playbooks indexed.
- **Credits**: If the bank signs an annual contract within 30 days of pilot completion, the pilot fee is credited against the first year's annual license cost.
