# 5A. ICP Definition

## ⚡ TL;DR
- The Phase 1 target is Large Private Indian Banks (INR 2,000 Cr+ revenue, 20+ internal legal headcount).
- Trigger events include RBI compliance updates, high litigation volumes, and internal directives to optimize legal spend.
- Cooperative banks and small regional NBFCs are classified as Anti-ICPs due to low budgets and limited transactional volume.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Firmographic Target Profile
- **Company Type**: Large Private Commercial Banks (e.g., Kotak, ICICI, HDFC, Axis, IndusInd).
- **Headcount**: 5,000+ total employees.
- **Legal Team Size**: 20 to 80 internal legal professionals (including litigation managers and contract reviewers).
- **Geography**: India (HQ in Mumbai, Bangalore, or Delhi-NCR).
- **Tech Maturity**: High. Has existing cloud infrastructure (AWS/Azure) or private container environments; utilizes basic document management systems.

### Trigger Events for Active Prospecting
1. **RBI Compliance Mandates**: Issuance of major compliance circulars requiring review of bank agreements.
2. **Litigation Headcount Freeze**: Directives to manage increasing litigation case volumes without hiring more lawyers.
3. **Budget Reviews**: Mandates to lower external panel counsel spend (outside counsel costs are the largest variable expense for legal departments).

### Anti-ICP (Who We Do NOT Target)
- **Cooperative & Regional Banks**: Lack IT infrastructure to host local VPCs; budgets are minimal.
- **Early NBFCs (< 50 employees)**: Low contract volume; simple templates; direct email communication is sufficient.
- **PSU Banks (Phase 1)**: Procurement cycles are governed by public tender laws, which can take 12-18 months and are high risk for early startups.
