# 5E. Revenue & Segment Expansion

## ⚡ TL;DR
- Land-and-expand strategies grow accounts by selling secondary modules (e.g., RBI Auditor) once the contract tool is adopted.
- Cross-segment expansion targets corporate consultancies and corporate law firms in Year 2.
- A 3-year revenue roadmap targets scaling ARR by expanding from banking to adjacent high-volume legal industries.

## 📖 Full Detail

### Land-and-Expand Playbook
1. **The Land (Litigation Tracking / Contract Review)**: Secure the initial sale using a high-pain module (e.g., eCourts tracker to prevent missed court dates).
2. **The Expand (Seat Expansion)**: Once litigation teams use the tool daily, expand seats to the transactional legal and credit teams.
3. **The Cross-Sell (Policy Auditor)**: Introduce the compliance and risk audit modules to the compliance department (CCO) for policy monitoring.

### Segment Expansion Map (Year 1 to Year 3)
```
[Year 1: Banking Legal (ICP)] ──► [Year 2: Law Firms & Corporate Advisories] ──► [Year 3: Large Auditing & Consultancy]
```

- **Year 1: Private Banks & NBFCs**: Establish baseline revenue, secure compliance certifications, and fine-tune models on Indian banking legal playbooks.
- **Year 2: Corporate Law Firms**: Package our contract highlighter as a standalone SaaS tool for legal firms handling M&A due diligence and commercial transactions.
- **Year 3: Large Audit & Consultancy Firms**: Target Big 4 and corporate consultancies. Sell high-volume compliance audit modules to automate risk checks across corporate portfolios.
