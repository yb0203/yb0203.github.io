# 5B. Go-To-Market (GTM) Motion

## ⚡ TL;DR
- Outbound sales leverage warm introductions via Indian alumni networks and Kotak internal connections.
- We utilize external law firms as referral partners to build trust with bank General Counsel.
- A 3-step outreach sequence is designed to secure discovery meetings with Chief Legal Officers.

## 🗺️ Where This Fits
[Layer 5 GTM → Feeds into Layer 5C Sales Cycle]

## 📖 Full Detail

### Multi-Channel GTM Strategy
1. **Direct Enterprise Outbound (Primary)**: Targeted outreach to CLOs, Head of Litigation, and Head of Legal Ops. Focus on the core value proposition: reducing manual contract review times.
2. **Referral Program via External Law Firms (Secondary)**: Law firms advising banks act as trusted recommenders. We partner with mid-sized Mumbai-based law firms, offering them workflow incentives to recommend Legal Owl to their bank clients.
3. **Warm Network Lever (Alumni & Banking Networks)**: Direct outreach via IIT-BHU alumni networks and existing professional connections inside Kotak Mahindra Bank to secure initial pilot reviews.

#### Step 2: The Proof Follow-up (LinkedIn / WhatsApp - Day 4)
