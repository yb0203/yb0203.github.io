## 1. Executive Summary & Core Positioning

Yashvi, you have an exceptionally strong **"Product-Minded Builder"** profile. Your combination of an elite technical education (IIT-BHU), high-scale fintech SDE experience at Kotak Mahindra Bank, and hands-on 0-to-1 AI builds (MCP, LangChain, RAG) puts you in the top tier of technical candidates.

However, a standard SDE resume emphasizes **execution details** (technologies used, code written). To land **PM** or **Founder's Office** roles, your resume and narrative must emphasize **discovery, alignment, leverage, and business outcomes**.

### The Positioning Pivot
```
[SDE / Engineering Persona] ──► [Product-Minded Builder / Founder's Office Persona]
- Focus on "What I built"      - Focus on "Why we built it & the business outcome"
- Highlighted technologies      - Highlighted user problems, operational bottlenecks, & scaling adoption
- Evaluated on code quality     - Evaluated on business leverage, velocity, and cross-functional leadership
```

## 3. Positioning Gaps & Areas of Improvement

### Gap 1: Engineering details overshadowing Product context
*   *Example*: "Led delivery of a mission-critical Cash Transaction capability across Finacle and TAS core systems... via ISO8583 and FIXML."
*   *Audit*: While technically impressive, a PM interviewer wants to know: What was the product's business value? How many users did it affect? What compliance bottleneck did you solve?
*   *Solution*: Frame it as leading a cross-functional launch of cash transaction systems that ensured regulatory compliance while maintaining high-throughput transaction reliability.

### Gap 2: Developer tooling framed as "test automation" instead of "internal product"
*   *Example*: "Designed a Gherkin-based BDD framework... scaled test infrastructure by driving adoption of self-serve CI standard..."
*   *Audit*: You basically acted as a Product Manager for internal Developer Experience (DevEx). SDEs and QAs were your "users."
*   *Solution*: Frame the BDD framework and CI standard as internal platforms that you successfully drove adoption for, highlighting the user adoption rate (e.g., "driving 100% adoption across 5+ engineering teams") and the developer productivity boost.

### Gap 3: Project descriptions lack "Why" and "User Feedback"
*   *Example*: GitaBae & Vibe Check.
*   *Audit*: They sound like side coding exercises. For PM/Founder's Office, we need to show that you thought about user acquisition, usability, and product market fit.
*   *Solution*: Discuss user traction (e.g., "500+ active sessions", "used by non-technical contributors to execute complex Git flows") or the specific UX problem solved (e.g., translating CLI complexity into plain English).
