## 🚀 Job Prep Roadmap & Strategy

1.  **Refine & Update Profiles**:
    *   Align your LinkedIn profile summary and experience descriptions with the positioning outlined in [resume_pm.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/resume_pm.md).
    *   Highlight your 0-to-1 project repositories on GitHub (GitaBae, Vibe Check) and pin them, adding clear readmes showing PM-oriented context (e.g., product decisions, AI safety considerations).

2.  **Practice Behavioral Stories**:
    *   Review [behavioral.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/interview_prep/behavioral.md).
    *   Practice describing these projects focusing heavily on *conflict/ambiguity resolution*, *aligning stakeholders (ops/compliance)*, and *quantifiable team metrics*.

3.  **Prepare for Cases & Tech Product Rounds**:
    *   Read [pm_cases.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/interview_prep/pm_cases.md) for templates.
    *   Ensure you can explain RAG, Pinecone, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) in simple terms, explaining the *product utility* and *market opportunity* behind them.

4.  **Track Applications**:
    *   Keep [tracker.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/applications/tracker.md) updated with target startups and companies.
    *   For Founder's Office roles, focus on cold emailing/DMing founders on Twitter/LinkedIn, writing brief 3-sentence high-agency pitches linking to your GitHub/MCP server project.
