## 🚀 Job Prep Roadmap & Strategy

1.  **Refine & Update Profiles**:
    *   Align your LinkedIn profile summary and experience descriptions with the positioning outlined in [pm_tailored.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/resumes/pm_tailored.md).
    *   Highlight your 0-to-1 project repositories on GitHub (Legal Owl, GitaBae, Vibe Check) and pin them, adding clear readmes showing PM-oriented context (e.g., product decisions, AI safety considerations).

2.  **Track Applications with the Job Board**:
    *   Use the Markdown Kanban board in [tracker.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/applications/tracker.md) to manage your pipeline.
    *   To progress a job, simply cut and paste the opportunity block from one section (e.g., **Target**) to another (e.g., **Applied** or **Interviewing**).
    *   Customize and use the templates in [outreach.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/applications/outreach.md) to reach out to founders and hiring managers.

3.  **Practice Behavioral Stories**:
    *   Review [behavioral.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/interview_prep/behavioral.md). Focus on explaining your engineering achievements in terms of *cross-functional leadership*, *operational efficiency*, and *AI trust/safety*.

4.  **Prepare for Cases & Tech Product Rounds**:
    *   Read [pm_cases.md](file:///Users/hkc/Documents/job-prep/interview_prep/pm_cases.md) for templates. Practice explaining RAG, MCP tools, and multi-agent human-in-the-loop gating ("approve-before-act") in simple product terms.
